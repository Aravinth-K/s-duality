The Schur index of 4d $\mathcal{N}=4$ SYM is a particular specialisation of the
superconformal index that counts a protected subset of local BPS operators. For
theories with simple gauge groups it may be described in terms of elliptic
hypergeometric integrals. S-duality predicts equality for $SO(2n+1)$ and
$Sp(2n)$ group theories, yet this is not manifest in the integral
representation. Here we present two proofs of this non-trivial duality, one for
the simpler unflavoured case, and one for the flavoured case.
